Section: Executive brief
Claim: UNITED should become a CAD/AVL-connected responder proximity layer, not a public responder-tracking app.
Source: User request; RapidSOS public-safety partner page; 911.gov NG911 definition.

Section: Operational concept
Claim: The system can give an assigned vehicle a trusted victim position, road distance, ETA, movement/staleness, and incident status while CAD remains authoritative.
Source: User request; APCO sample RFP guidance for CAD/AVL; RapidSOS Digital Alerts.

Section: System architecture
Claim: A practical architecture joins UNITED alert data, RapidSOS/ECC intake, CAD incident assignment, agency AVL, routing, and an MDT interface through an authenticated incident broker.
Source: RapidSOS partner materials; 911.gov NG911 playbook; FirstNet situational-awareness roadmap.

Section: In-vehicle experience
Claim: A driver-safe proximity view must be glanceable, assignment-scoped, and explicit about location accuracy and stale data.
Source: User request; U.S. DOT driver-distraction guidance; NOPD MDC operational guidance.

Section: Data and integration contract
Claim: Stable IDs, timestamped coordinates, accuracy metadata, assignment status, and auditable event transitions are the minimum trustworthy data contract.
Source: APCO/NENA EIDD; APCO status-code standard; 911.gov data-sharing plan.

Section: Security and governance
Claim: Vehicle and victim locations require agency-owned identity, least privilege, encryption, retention limits, and no public access.
Source: FBI CJIS Security Policy; NIST SP 800-207 principles; public-safety norms.

Section: Pilot plan
Claim: A shadow-mode pilot with one agency and one CAD vendor should validate workflow and data quality before any live operational dependency.
Source: 911.gov phased NG911 guidance; engineering best practice.

Section: Verification and acceptance
Claim: The pilot should be accepted only on measured location freshness, availability, handoff integrity, driver usability, security, and safe degradation.
Source: APCO RFP guidance; user request.

Section: RapidSOS discussion proposal
Claim: The immediate ask is a technical discovery and sandbox path, not access to live responder locations.
Source: RapidSOS public-safety software partner and partner-network pages.

Section: Sources
Claim: The proposal is grounded in published RapidSOS, 911.gov, APCO, FirstNet, FBI, and DOT material, with local validation still required.
Source: Linked references in document.
